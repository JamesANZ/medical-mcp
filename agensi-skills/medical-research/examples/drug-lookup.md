# Drug lookup

User: What does the FDA label say about ibuprofen, and is there a shortage record?

1. `search-drugs` with `query` `ibuprofen` and `countries` `["US"]`.
2. `search-drug-safety` for shortages and recalls on the same name.
3. Report the label title, identifier, and link from the tool. State that the result is source text, not a dose recommendation.
